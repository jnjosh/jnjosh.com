I bought a new fuzz pedal, [EHX Big Muff Pi 2](https://www.ehx.com/products/big-muff-pi-2/), and wanted to make a song with it. The other interesting thing is I used the out of my Yamaha THR30II into Logic to record the fuzz guitar parts.

I'm still incredibly new to this but this might be the first time that I was able to get what I imagined in my head recorded, so I'm happy with that.
