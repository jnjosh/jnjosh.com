Another find while wondering around the [caruggi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_center_of_Genoa) of Genoa, Chiesa di San Matteo. The church has alternating horizontal lines of black slate and white marble and is quite nestled in the city. It was a real surprise turning a corner and suddenly you are in Piazza San Matteo.
