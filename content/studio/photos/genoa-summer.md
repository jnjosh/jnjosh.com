Walking around Genoa and just seeing amazing things down the [caruggi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_center_of_Genoa) was one of my favorite things. This was a regular occurrence where we'd walk by and just see some amazing view or building or event nice shadows. The caruggi were so easy to get lost in. I could spend more time there doing just photography.

The reminder that it was super hot our entire time there too—which was not one of my favorite things. This made it really hard to want to do all the photography. I'd end each day just completely beat up, and partially dehydrated. Thankfully our apartment had A/C.
